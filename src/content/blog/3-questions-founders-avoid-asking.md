In early-stage enterprise sales, founders often fear asking hard questions because they subconsciously worry the answer might derail an otherwise pleasant conversation.

Pleasant conversations do not close six-figure contracts. Clear diagnostic truth does.

### 1. "What happens if you do absolutely nothing for the next six months?"

If the answer is *"We'll probably manage okay,"* you do not have an active deal—you have an educational chat.

### 2. "Who inside your organisation will advocate against making this investment?"

Every purchase has an internal dissenter. If your prospect hasn't identified them yet, they will encounter them the moment you send a contract.

### 3. "What makes solving this problem a priority this quarter versus next year?"

Urgency cannot be manufactured with artificial discounts. Real urgency is anchored in existing executive pain.
