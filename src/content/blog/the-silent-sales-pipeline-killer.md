Most sales leaders audit conversion rates by looking at lost reasons: *price, timing, budget, internal reprioritization*.

In reality, these are almost never the true causes.

### The Real Culprit: The Decision Fog

When a high-value B2B buyer goes dark or offers a polite postponement, what actually occurred was an unaddressed pocket of ambiguity:

1. **Risk Ambiguity:** Will choosing this solution get me blamed if it fails?
2. **Implementation Ambiguity:** Do we have the bandwidth to actually install and run this?
3. **Certainty Ambiguity:** Is the outcome guaranteed or merely promised?

### The Tactical Prescription

Before ending any critical discovery or demo call, never ask *"Does that all make sense?"*

Instead, ask:

> **"If we were to move forward today, what is the single biggest internal friction point or uncertainty your team would have to solve first?"**

This forces hidden objections into the daylight where they can be resolved systematically.
